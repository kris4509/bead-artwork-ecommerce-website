Our Story

Asili Creations started as a creative side hustle between friends who shared a deep passion for handmade crafts and Kenyan artistry.

What began as a small project to create unique gifts for family has grown into a mission: to bring the authentic beauty of traditional beadwork into modern homes across Kenya.

We blend heritage with contemporary design to create pieces that tell a story on your table.
